Discord Music Bot - Commands
Full list of available commands for the music bot.
Playlist & Stations
!load_playlist <URL> — Load a YouTube/YT Music playlist into the queue
!play [URL or search] — Start queue, queue a URL, or search & queue a song
!play_now <URL or search> — Immediately play a song, interrupting current track
!live_play <playlist URL> — Load & play a playlist in live concert mode (no jingles)
!midnight_podcast — Load the wuwa Midnight Podcast playlist
!thairmx — Load the Thai Remix playlist
Playback Controls
!skip [count] — Skip current song (or multiple songs)
!repeat — Toggle repeat for the current song
!repeat_playlist — Toggle repeat for the last loaded playlist
!stop — Clear queue, stop playback, and disconnect
!test_jingle — Play the configured jingle in your voice channel
Queue
!queue — Show current track and next 10 songs in queue
Images
!wuwa — Browse Wuthering Waves character art (SFW/NSFW picker)
!zani — Get a random Zani image from Danbooru
!zanisafe — Get a random Zani image from Safebooru
